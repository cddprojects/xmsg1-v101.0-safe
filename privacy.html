Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how FindVerge collects, uses and discloses personal data when you visit this site or send us an enquiry. We handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through our form, we collect your name and email address. You may also add a message, which is optional. Please do not include your NRIC number, bank details or passwords in your message.

Why we collect it

We use this information only to reply to your enquiry and to follow up on it. By submitting the form, you consent to us collecting, using and disclosing your personal data for this purpose. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, although we may then be unable to reply to your enquiry.

When you browse the site

You can read the guide and download the tracker without an account. This site does not currently use analytics tags or advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may process technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Who handles your enquiry

Our hosting, form delivery and email providers may process your enquiry so that we can receive and reply to it. Some of these providers may store or process data outside Singapore. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as we need it to handle your enquiry and any follow-up, or as required by law. After that, we delete it or anonymise it.

The job search tracker

The tracker is a downloadable CSV file. FindVerge does not receive the information you enter into your own copy.

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, including government websites. Those websites handle personal data under their own policies.

Your rights and how to contact us

You may ask us for access to, or correction of, the personal data we hold about you, and you may withdraw your consent. Use the enquiry form and mark your message as a data protection request. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Personal Data Protection Commission.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
